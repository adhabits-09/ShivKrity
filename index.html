Our story

We did not come from a traditional technical background. Like many beginners, we started learning digital work, online tools, content creation, marketing and various digital skills from scratch — asking the same questions most people start with: what should we learn, where do we begin, and which skills are actually practical?

We learned gradually, practiced consistently, made our share of mistakes, and kept growing through experience. Today we've trained and guided 700+ people, and we're still moving forward in our own digital journey.

"Our journey was never just about money — it was about learning, working and growing while staying close to family."

Many couples want to stay with their children, parents and loved ones, but career and financial pressure often pushes people to move away in search of opportunity. We understand that reality closely — it's exactly why we chose to seriously learn and apply digital skills in our own lives.

After nearly a year of continuous learning, practice and work, our situation looks very different from where we started. We're no longer dependent on moving away from family just to earn a living — building digital skills gave us a path to work and grow while staying right where our family is. For us, that's one of the biggest wins of this journey.
